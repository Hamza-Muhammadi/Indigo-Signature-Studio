# Indigo Signature Studio

A free email signature builder by **Ameer Hamza**.

[Open the builder](https://hamza-muhammadi.github.io/Indigo-Signature-Studio/)

## Use it
1. Enter your details and links.
2. Upload your logo or use initials.
3. Copy your signature into your email signature settings.
4. Choose your defaults, save and send a test email.

## Run locally
Download this repository and open `index.html`, or serve it with `python -m http.server 8000`. No install, API key or account required. Clipboard support depends on browser permissions and secure context.

## Publish your copy
Fork the repository. In Settings → Pages, choose Deploy from a branch, main, / (root), then Save. Change the demo logo URL in index.html to your own hosted image.

## Privacy and compatibility
Form details and uploaded images stay in browser local storage. Clearing site data removes them. Public image URLs request images from their hosts. No analytics, email access or server-side form storage is included. Email apps may change styles or strip embedded images. Insert uploaded photos through your email editor if needed. Test before relying on it.

## Customise
Edit index.html for the layout and defaults, app.js for the signature renderer. builder.html is the standalone download and must be regenerated after editing.
