FROM PREVIEW TO INBOX

One setup. Every introduction.

1. Copy your signature

Add your details and click Copy signature. Use a desktop browser for setup.

2. Open Gmail settings

Settings → See all settings → General → Signature → Create new. Paste into the editor.

3. Save and test

Select it for new emails and replies, then Save Changes. Send yourself a test email.

Will it look the same in every email app?

Email apps can adjust colours, fonts and spacing. Test on your phone and desktop. If an uploaded image disappears, insert it using your email editor’s image button.

Where are my details saved?

Your details and uploaded image are stored in this browser. No account or inbox access is required. Public image URLs load from their hosting provider. Clear the demo or browser site data when using a shared computer.
